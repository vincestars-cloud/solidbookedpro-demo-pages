Welcome to GreenScape Landscaping

GreenScape Landscaping is a locally owned business with 15 years of experience delivering high-quality outdoor work. We care as much about our crews as our customers, and that shows up in the finish. As a single point of contact for design, build and maintenance, we keep your project simple to manage from first walk-through to final clean-up.

Experts in our industry

Our teams deliver landscape design and installation, lawn care and maintenance, hardscaping and patios, irrigation, tree and shrub care, and seasonal cleanups. Doing it all in-house means one accountable crew, one schedule and one standard of finish across your property.

Nurturing talent to delight our customers

Our business is our people. We invest in training and keep our crews long-term, because the same familiar faces on your property every visit is what turns a job into a relationship. That consistency is why most of our work comes from referrals and repeat customers.

Landscape Design & Build

Full design and installation of planting, lawns, borders and outdoor living areas, built by an experienced crew and finished to last.

Lawn Care & Maintenance

Scheduled mowing, edging, fertilization and weed control that keeps your property sharp week after week, on a route you can set your watch by.

Hardscaping & Patios

Patios, walkways, retaining walls and fire pits — hand-laid paver and stone work engineered to drain properly and stay level for decades.

Irrigation & Sprinklers

Tree & Shrub Care

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring and fall cleanups, leaf removal, mulching and bed refreshes that reset your property at every change of season.
