Rc's Lawn care FAQs

  • What services does Rc's Lawn care provide?

    Rc's Lawn care does it all – landscaping design, plant installation, drainage solutions, hardscaping, lawn care, leaf removal, snow removal, and Christmas light installation. Our services protect your property by removing debris, eliminating risks like slippery sidewalks, preventing pests and disease, and improving your curb appeal.

  • Does Rc's Lawn care provide free quotes?

    Yes! Rc's Lawn care provides free quotes for landscaping services in Fayetteville and beyond. We'll listen to your needs and work together to understand the scope of your project, communicating clearly every step of the way. You'll receive a detailed quote for services that will make your property look its best!

  • Does Rc's Lawn care provide lawn maintenance?

    Yes, Rc's Lawn care provides ongoing lawn maintenance and care. Our packages include year-round care like aeration, overseeding, fertilizing, disease control, and weed prevention. We're here to help if you need regular maintenance or a one-off service.

  • What is landscaping vs. hardscaping?

    Landscaping traditionally refers to softscaping, or changing the layout of an exterior through plant installation, mulching, and lawn care. Hardscaping, on the other hand, involves building structures out of wood or stone that can improve drainage and usage of the space. This includes retaining walls, firepits, decks, and more. At Rc's Lawn care, we're experts in all things exterior—whether you need to freshen up your landscaping or install some hardscaping.

  • What areas does Rc's Lawn care serve?
